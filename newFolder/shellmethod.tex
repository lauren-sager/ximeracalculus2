\begin{abstract}
   Working with the peers in your group, solve the following problems. Make sure to show and justify all your work. Make sure everyone in the group understands the solution and participates. Be prepared to report your answers to the whole class.

\maketitle
\textbf{Purpose: }To explore the volumes of revolution. This activity focuses on building intuition and confidence with the volume of revolution construction discussed in lecture. You will work with your groups to find volumes of solids of rotation using cylindrical shells.\\

\begin{exercise}
    Find the volume of the solid generated by $g(x)=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^3}}$ from $[1,4]$ rotated about the $y$-axis.
\end{exercise}

\begin{exercise}
    Find the volume of the solid generated by rotating the area enclosed by $f(x)=6-x$, $g(x)=3x-2$ and $x=0$ rotated about the $y$-axis.  
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Calculate the area using the shell method.
        \item Calculate the area using the disk method.
        \item Reflect on the two methods. Which method is easier for this problem? Why?
    \end{enumerate}
\end{exercise}
